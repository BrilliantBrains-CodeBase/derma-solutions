Anti-Ageing Treatments for Men: What Is Different?

Introduction: Men Age Differently—So Should Their Anti-Ageing Treatments

Over the past few years, I’ve noticed an interesting shift in my dermatology practice.

More men are walking into the clinic—not because someone else encouraged them, but because they genuinely want to take better care of their skin.

One of the first questions they usually ask is,

“Doctor, are anti-ageing treatments only for women?”

The answer is simple.

Absolutely not.

Healthy skin has nothing to do with gender.

Men age too.

They develop wrinkles.

Their skin loses collagen.

They experience pigmentation, sun damage, sagging, and dullness just like women do.

What is different is how men’s skin ages and how we treat it.

At Derma Solutions in Marathahalli, Bengaluru, I meet professionals from Whitefield, Bellandur, Sarjapur Road, Electronic City, HSR Layout, Indiranagar, and Koramangala every week.

Many are software engineers.

Some are entrepreneurs.

Others are doctors, lawyers, consultants, fitness professionals, or business owners.

Most of them have one thing in common.

They aren’t trying to look twenty again.

They simply want to look energetic.

Confident.

Well-rested.

Healthy.

They tell me things like,

“People keep asking if I’m stressed.”

“I look older on video calls.”

“My face looks tired even after a good night’s sleep.”

“I’ve started noticing deep forehead lines.”

“My jawline isn’t as sharp as it used to be.”

These concerns are completely normal.

The good news is that modern dermatology offers several safe, effective, and natural-looking solutions.

But before choosing any treatment, it’s important to understand one fact.

Men’s skin is biologically different from women’s skin.

That means treatment planning should also be different.

Let’s understand why.

Do Men Really Age Differently?

Yes.

And the difference begins at the microscopic level.

Many people assume skincare products and cosmetic treatments work the same for everyone.

In reality, men’s skin has unique structural characteristics that influence how it ages.

Understanding these differences helps dermatologists create personalised treatment plans instead of simply following trends.

How Is Men’s Skin Different?

Several biological factors influence male skin.

1. Men’s Skin Is Thicker

Studies show that men’s skin is generally around 20–25% thicker than women’s skin.

This increased thickness is mainly due to testosterone.

Thicker skin often means:

  • Stronger collagen network
  • Firmer skin in younger years
  • Slower appearance of early fine lines

However, this doesn’t mean men don’t age.

It simply means the ageing pattern is different.

2. Men Produce More Oil

Men typically have larger sebaceous glands.

As a result, they produce more sebum.

This has both advantages and disadvantages.

Benefits include:

  • Better natural skin hydration
  • Slightly delayed appearance of superficial wrinkles

Challenges include:

  • Oily skin
  • Enlarged pores
  • Acne
  • Acne scars
  • Increased shine
  • Blackheads

This is why many male patients continue experiencing acne well into adulthood.

3. Facial Hair Changes Skin Health

Beards affect skincare in several ways.

Regular shaving can:

  • Exfoliate the skin
  • Cause razor burns
  • Lead to ingrown hairs
  • Trigger pigmentation
  • Irritate sensitive skin

Patients with dense beards also require different treatment planning when considering procedures like laser resurfacing or skin tightening.

4. Collagen Declines Differently

One interesting difference is collagen loss.

Women often experience accelerated collagen decline after menopause because of hormonal changes.

In men, collagen generally decreases more gradually.

However, once ageing becomes visible, changes may appear more dramatic because men’s facial muscles are stronger and facial expressions create deeper dynamic wrinkles.

Why Are More Men Seeking Anti-Ageing Treatments Today?

Ten years ago, aesthetic dermatology was often viewed as something primarily for women.

Today, that’s changing rapidly.

Modern men understand that looking healthy and confident is part of professional and personal well-being.

Many of my patients aren’t interested in dramatic makeovers.

They’re looking for subtle improvements.

Reasons men seek treatment include:

  • High-definition video meetings
  • Leadership and client-facing roles
  • Increased awareness through social media
  • Better understanding of skincare
  • Wedding preparation
  • Career advancement
  • Personal confidence

Looking refreshed isn’t about vanity.

It’s about presenting yourself the way you feel inside.

What Are the First Signs of Ageing in Men?

Most men don’t notice ageing overnight.

The changes develop gradually.

The first visible signs usually include:

Forehead Lines

Repeated facial expressions slowly create horizontal forehead wrinkles.

These are often among the earliest ageing concerns.

Frown Lines

The vertical lines between the eyebrows become deeper over time.

These can make someone appear angry or stressed even when relaxed.

Crow’s Feet

Fine lines around the eyes develop with smiling, squinting, and sun exposure.

Pigmentation

Years of sun exposure without sunscreen often lead to:

  • Uneven skin tone
  • Sunspots
  • Tanning
  • Age spots

Many men begin sunscreen only after pigmentation becomes noticeable.

Dull Skin

Busy schedules.

Poor sleep.

Stress.

Pollution.

Long working hours.

These factors reduce skin radiance over time.

Loss of Jawline Definition

As collagen decreases, the lower face gradually changes.

Many men notice:

  • Early jowls
  • Loose skin
  • Reduced facial definition

This is often one of the biggest concerns among patients in their forties and fifties.

What Causes Premature Ageing in Men?

Ageing is natural.

Premature ageing is often preventable.

Several lifestyle factors accelerate the process.

Sun Exposure

If I could identify one habit responsible for most premature skin ageing, it would be inadequate sun protection.

Many men rarely use sunscreen.

Years of cumulative UV exposure gradually damage collagen.

The result?

Wrinkles.

Pigmentation.

Loss of elasticity.

Uneven skin texture.

Sun protection is one of the simplest and most effective anti-ageing strategies available.

Smoking

Smoking affects blood circulation.

It damages collagen.

It slows wound healing.

It contributes to dullness and deeper wrinkles.

Smokers often show signs of ageing earlier than non-smokers.

Poor Sleep

Your skin repairs itself during sleep.

Chronic sleep deprivation affects:

  • Skin barrier function
  • Hydration
  • Inflammation
  • Dark circles
  • Overall skin quality

Stress

Many professionals in Bengaluru lead demanding lives.

Long work hours.

Deadlines.

Business pressure.

Stress increases cortisol levels, which can worsen skin ageing, acne, and inflammation.

Pollution

Urban pollution exposes the skin to free radicals that contribute to oxidative stress.

Over time, this affects collagen and skin health.

Poor Skincare Habits

Some men still believe washing the face with soap is enough.

Others use products that are too harsh.

Healthy skin requires a consistent, evidence-based skincare routine—not dozens of products, but the right ones.

When Should Men Start Anti-Ageing Treatments?

This is probably the most common question I hear.

The answer isn’t based on age.

It’s based on skin condition.

Some men in their late twenties already have significant sun damage.

Others in their forties have remarkably healthy skin.

Rather than asking,

“How old am I?”

Ask,

“What does my skin need today?”

For most men:

In Your 20s

Focus on prevention.

  • Daily sunscreen
  • Gentle cleanser
  • Moisturiser
  • Vitamin C
  • Retinoids when appropriate
  • Healthy lifestyle habits

In Your 30s

This is when many men begin noticing:

  • Early wrinkles
  • Pigmentation
  • Mild volume loss
  • Reduced skin brightness

Professional skincare and collagen-stimulating treatments may become appropriate depending on individual concerns.

In Your 40s and Beyond

Ageing becomes more structural.

Patients may benefit from a personalised combination of:

  • Injectable treatments
  • Skin tightening procedures
  • Laser treatments
  • Medical-grade skincare
  • Lifestyle optimisation

The key is not to chase youth.

The goal is to age well.

Prevention Is Always Better Than Correction

One of the biggest misconceptions is that anti-ageing treatment begins when wrinkles become deep.

In reality, dermatologists prefer to protect collagen before significant damage occurs.

Small, timely interventions often produce the most natural long-term outcomes.

The earlier you begin protecting your skin, the easier it becomes to maintain healthy ageing.

Why Anti-Ageing Treatments for Men Need a Different Approach

One of the biggest misconceptions about cosmetic dermatology is that the same treatment plan works for everyone.

It doesn’t.

While men and women may undergo many of the same procedures, the way those treatments are performed is often very different.

The reason is simple.

Men have different facial anatomy.

Different muscle strength.

Different skin thickness.

Different ageing patterns.

And most importantly, different aesthetic goals.

In my practice, male patients rarely say,

“Doctor, I want to look younger.”

Instead, they usually say,

“I want to look less tired.”

“I don’t want people asking if I’m stressed.”

“I want to look confident during meetings.”

“I don’t want to look artificial.”

That last point is especially important.

Most men are looking for subtle improvements that preserve their natural masculine appearance.

The goal isn’t transformation.

It’s restoration.

Botox for Men: More Than Just Wrinkle Reduction

Botox is one of the most popular anti-ageing treatments among men today.

Yet it’s also one of the most misunderstood.

Many people worry that Botox will leave them expressionless or give them an unnatural appearance.

When performed correctly, that shouldn’t happen.

Botox works by temporarily relaxing specific facial muscles responsible for dynamic wrinkles.

It is commonly used to improve:

  • Horizontal forehead lines
  • Frown lines between the eyebrows
  • Crow’s feet around the eyes
  • Bunny lines on the nose
  • Chin dimpling
  • Jaw muscle enlargement (in selected patients)

For men, however, treatment planning is different.

Men Usually Have Stronger Facial Muscles

Male facial muscles are generally larger and stronger than female facial muscles.

As a result, men often require:

  • Different dosing
  • Different injection patterns
  • Different treatment goals

The objective is not to erase every wrinkle.

A few natural expression lines often suit a masculine face.

Instead, we soften harsh lines while maintaining normal facial movement.

A good Botox treatment should never make someone look “treated.”

People should simply notice that you look well-rested.

Dermal Fillers: Restoring Structure, Not Changing Identity

Dermal fillers are another treatment that is often misunderstood.

Many associate fillers only with fuller lips or dramatic facial changes.

In reality, fillers play an important role in restoring facial balance.

As men age, they gradually lose:

  • Facial fat
  • Bone support
  • Skin elasticity
  • Collagen

This can lead to:

  • Hollow cheeks
  • Deep smile lines
  • Under-eye hollowness
  • Loss of jawline definition
  • A tired appearance

Dermal fillers help restore lost support.

For male patients, however, restraint is essential.

The goal is not to create softer or rounder facial contours.

Instead, fillers are used to maintain masculine facial proportions.

Treatment may focus on:

  • Jawline definition
  • Chin projection
  • Mid-face support
  • Tear trough correction in selected patients

Natural results always remain the priority.

Skin Boosters: Healthy Skin Without Changing Facial Features

Not every patient needs injectables that alter facial structure.

Some simply want healthier skin.

Skin boosters are designed for exactly that purpose.

Unlike fillers, skin boosters improve overall skin quality rather than adding volume.

Benefits may include:

  • Better hydration
  • Improved skin texture
  • Enhanced skin elasticity
  • Brighter complexion
  • Reduced fine crepey lines

Many professionals prefer skin boosters because colleagues notice healthier skin—not cosmetic treatment.

HIFU: Non-Surgical Skin Tightening for Men

As men reach their forties and fifties, one common concern is facial sagging.

Patients often notice:

  • Reduced jawline definition
  • Early jowls
  • Loose neck skin
  • Mild cheek descent

For carefully selected patients, HIFU (High-Intensity Focused Ultrasound) can help stimulate collagen production deep beneath the skin.

It is commonly used for:

  • Jawline tightening
  • Neck tightening
  • Brow lifting
  • Mild facial lifting

HIFU is particularly attractive for busy professionals because there is little to no downtime.

However, expectations should remain realistic.

HIFU cannot replace a surgical facelift.

It is most effective for early to moderate skin laxity.

Radiofrequency Skin Tightening

Radiofrequency (RF) treatments use controlled heat energy to stimulate collagen remodeling.

Many men appreciate RF because treatment sessions are comfortable and involve minimal recovery time.

RF may improve:

  • Fine wrinkles
  • Mild skin laxity
  • Skin texture
  • Facial firmness

For some patients, RF is combined with microneedling to enhance collagen stimulation.

Combination treatments often produce better long-term results than relying on a single procedure alone.

Fractional CO₂ Laser: Rebuilding Collagen From Within

One of the most effective procedures for improving skin texture and stimulating collagen is Fractional CO₂ Laser.

This treatment creates controlled microscopic injuries that trigger the skin’s natural healing process.

Over time, new collagen develops.

Fractional CO₂ Laser may help improve:

  • Acne scars
  • Fine wrinkles
  • Enlarged pores
  • Sun damage
  • Uneven skin texture
  • Mild skin laxity

Because recovery involves temporary redness and peeling, careful planning is important.

The procedure should always be performed after proper evaluation by an experienced dermatologist.

Chemical Peels for Men

Many people think chemical peels are designed only for women.

That is not true.

Chemical peels are frequently recommended for male patients experiencing:

  • Pigmentation
  • Acne
  • Post-acne marks
  • Dull skin
  • Sun damage
  • Uneven skin tone

The appropriate peel depends on:

  • Skin type
  • Pigmentation tendency
  • Medical history
  • Lifestyle
  • Treatment goals

Professional guidance is especially important for Indian skin, where inappropriate peels may increase the risk of post-inflammatory pigmentation.

A Simple Anti-Ageing Skincare Routine for Men

One of the biggest myths I hear is:

“Doctor, I don’t have time for a skincare routine.”

Fortunately, maintaining healthy skin does not require ten different products.

A simple, consistent routine is often far more effective.

Morning

  • Gentle cleanser
  • Vitamin C serum (if recommended)
  • Moisturiser
  • Broad-spectrum sunscreen (SPF 30 or higher)

Night

  • Gentle cleanser
  • Retinoid (if suitable)
  • Moisturiser

That’s it.

Consistency matters more than complexity.

Common Myths About Anti-Ageing Treatments for Men

Myth 1: Cosmetic Treatments Are Only for Women

False.

Healthy skin has no gender.

An increasing number of men now seek dermatologist-guided aesthetic care.

Myth 2: Botox Makes Everyone Look Frozen

Not when performed correctly.

The goal is softer, more relaxed facial expressions—not loss of movement.

Myth 3: Fillers Always Look Obvious

Poorly planned fillers look obvious.

Well-executed treatments simply restore natural facial balance.

Myth 4: Anti-Ageing Treatments Are Only Needed After 50

Preventive skincare often begins much earlier.

Early intervention generally requires less treatment than correcting advanced ageing later.

Myth 5: Expensive Products Are Always Better

Price does not guarantee effectiveness.

Evidence-based ingredients recommended by a dermatologist are far more important than luxury branding.

Combination Treatments Often Deliver the Best Results

One treatment rarely addresses every aspect of ageing.

For many male patients, the best outcomes come from combining different approaches.

A personalised plan may include:

  • Medical-grade skincare
  • Daily sunscreen
  • Botox for dynamic wrinkles
  • Skin boosters for hydration
  • HIFU for skin tightening
  • Fractional laser for texture improvement
  • Chemical peels for pigmentation

Each treatment addresses a different aspect of skin ageing.

Together, they help create natural, balanced improvements without dramatically changing appearance.

I try to help them look healthier.

More refreshed.

More confident.

A successful anti-ageing treatment should never make people ask,

“Did you get something done?”

Lifestyle Habits That Help Men Age Better

One of the biggest misconceptions about anti-ageing is that it begins in a dermatology clinic.

In reality, it begins with your daily habits.

No laser.

No injectable.

No skincare product can completely overcome years of poor lifestyle choices.

Healthy skin is the result of consistent care, both inside and out.

Here are the habits I recommend to every male patient.

1. Never Skip Sunscreen

If I could recommend only one anti-ageing product, it would be sunscreen.

Many men still believe sunscreen is only necessary for beach vacations or outdoor sports.

That isn’t true.

Daily exposure to ultraviolet (UV) radiation while driving, commuting, walking outdoors, or sitting near windows gradually damages collagen.

Over time, this contributes to:

  • Fine lines
  • Pigmentation
  • Uneven skin tone
  • Sun spots
  • Loss of skin elasticity

A broad-spectrum sunscreen with SPF 30 or higher should become part of your everyday routine.

2. Sleep Is Skin Recovery Time

During sleep, your skin repairs itself.

Poor sleep affects more than your energy levels.

It also impacts:

  • Skin hydration
  • Barrier function
  • Collagen repair
  • Dark circles
  • Overall skin quality

Aim for at least seven to eight hours of quality sleep each night.

3. Eat for Your Skin

There is no miracle anti-ageing diet.

However, a balanced diet rich in antioxidants, vitamins, healthy fats, and lean protein supports healthier skin.

Foods that benefit skin include:

  • Colourful fruits
  • Green leafy vegetables
  • Nuts and seeds
  • Omega-3-rich fish
  • Eggs
  • Lentils
  • Fresh salads

Reducing excessive sugar intake may also help protect collagen over time.

4. Stay Physically Active

Exercise improves blood circulation, reduces stress, and supports overall skin health.

Regular physical activity also helps maintain muscle tone, body composition, and confidence—all of which contribute to a healthier appearance.

5. Stop Smoking

Smoking is one of the fastest ways to accelerate skin ageing.

It damages collagen, reduces blood supply to the skin, delays healing, and contributes to deeper wrinkles.

Quitting smoking benefits not only your skin but your overall health.

6. Manage Stress

Stress has become part of modern life, especially among working professionals.

Chronic stress increases cortisol levels, which can contribute to:

  • Premature ageing
  • Acne
  • Hair fall
  • Inflammation
  • Poor skin barrier function

Simple habits like regular exercise, meditation, hobbies, and adequate rest can make a meaningful difference.

Common Skincare Mistakes Men Make

Over the years, I have noticed certain patterns among male patients.

Many of these habits unintentionally speed up skin ageing.

Using Soap Instead of a Gentle Cleanser

Traditional bathing soaps are often too harsh for facial skin.

They may strip away natural oils and weaken the skin barrier.

A dermatologist-recommended facial cleanser is a better choice.

Ignoring Moisturiser

Many men believe moisturisers are only for dry skin.

In reality, every skin type benefits from appropriate hydration.

Even oily skin requires a lightweight moisturiser.

Using Too Many Products at Once

Social media has encouraged people to layer multiple active ingredients without understanding how they interact.

This can cause:

  • Irritation
  • Redness
  • Barrier damage
  • Increased sensitivity

A simple, consistent routine is usually more effective.

Delaying Professional Advice

Some patients wait until wrinkles become deep or skin sagging becomes significant before consulting a dermatologist.

Earlier intervention often allows for more conservative, natural-looking treatment.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

1. Are anti-ageing treatments becoming more popular among men?

Yes.

More men are seeking dermatologist-guided treatments to improve skin health, reduce signs of ageing, and maintain a refreshed appearance while preserving natural masculine features.

2. At what age should men start anti-ageing treatments?

There is no fixed age.

Preventive skincare should ideally begin in the twenties, while procedures such as Botox, skin boosters, or collagen-stimulating treatments are recommended based on individual skin concerns rather than age alone.

3. Is Botox different for men?

Yes.

Men usually have stronger facial muscles and different facial proportions, which often require customised injection techniques and dosing.

The aim is to soften wrinkles while maintaining natural facial expressions.

4. Can men get dermal fillers without looking unnatural?

Absolutely.

When performed by an experienced dermatologist, fillers restore facial support and definition without creating an artificial appearance.

5. Which anti-ageing treatment is best for men?

There is no single best treatment.

The right option depends on your skin condition, age, lifestyle, and aesthetic goals.

A dermatologist can recommend a personalised treatment plan.

6. Is sunscreen really necessary for men?

Yes.

Daily sunscreen is one of the most effective ways to prevent premature skin ageing, pigmentation, and UV-related collagen damage.

7. Can men benefit from HIFU?

Yes.

HIFU may improve mild skin laxity, jawline definition, and neck tightening in appropriately selected patients.

8. Are anti-ageing treatments painful?

Most modern procedures involve minimal discomfort.

Topical numbing creams and advanced techniques help improve patient comfort.

9. How long do anti-ageing treatment results last?

Results vary depending on the procedure.

Some treatments require maintenance every few months, while collagen-stimulating procedures provide gradual improvements over a longer period.

10. Do anti-ageing treatments make men look feminine?

No.

Proper treatment planning respects masculine facial anatomy and focuses on preserving natural facial structure.

11. Can lifestyle changes reduce signs of ageing?

Yes.

Healthy nutrition, sun protection, quality sleep, regular exercise, stress management, and avoiding smoking all contribute to healthier-looking skin.

12. Can anti-ageing treatments improve confidence?

Many patients report feeling more confident after treatment because they look healthier, more energetic, and less tired—not because they look dramatically different.

13. Are laser treatments suitable for men?

Yes.

Laser treatments can improve pigmentation, acne scars, skin texture, enlarged pores, and signs of photoageing in suitable candidates.

14. How often should men visit a dermatologist?

Even if you have no major concerns, an annual skin assessment is beneficial.

Patients undergoing treatment may require periodic follow-up visits depending on their personalised care plan.

15. How do I know which treatment is right for me?

The best treatment is determined after a comprehensive skin evaluation by a qualified dermatologist who considers your skin type, facial anatomy, medical history, and treatment goals.
