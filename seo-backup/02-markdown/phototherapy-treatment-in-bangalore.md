## What is Phototherapy Treatment?

An advanced light-based solution for treating skin disorders.

![Phototherapy](https://dermasolutions.co.in/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/Phototherapy-depo.jpg)

Phototherapy, also known as light therapy, is a non-invasive medical procedure that uses specific wavelengths of ultraviolet (UV) light to treat various skin conditions.

This FDA-approved treatment is highly effective in managing chronic skin disorders like vitiligo, psoriasis, atopic dermatitis, and even certain types of skin cancer.

By targeting affected areas with controlled UV light, phototherapy helps reduce inflammation, improve pigmentation, and promote healthier skin.

## How Does Phototherapy Treatment Work?

The science behind effective light-based therapy.

Phototherapy uses controlled exposure to specific wavelengths of ultraviolet (UV) light—either UVA or UVB.

These light waves penetrate the skin’s surface, targeting affected areas without damaging surrounding tissues. By modulating the immune response and reducing inflammation, phototherapy promotes skin healing and regeneration.

NARROW BAND ULTRAVIOLET B (NB-UVB) PHOTOTHERAPY uses 290-320 nm wavelength, which is the best type of phototherapy treatment for vitiligo.

These units can be used for very small localized areas of white vitiliginous patches like lips, fingertips, hands and feet to whole-body generalised vitiligo by using whole body chambers.

At **Derma Solutions**, we have whole body NB UVB PHOTOTHERAPY chamber to treat whole body vitiligo, Psoriasis etc. .

The unit is equipped with NBUVB lamps micro computerised electronic controller with LCD display. Optimum dose for each indication corresponds to 0.7-0.9 MED (minimal erythema dose) and depending upon skin type dose of irradiation is adjusted by the doctor and overall time for each treatment. Approx 3-4 minutes for each area is advised and incremental dosing is done every session depending upon patients feedback.

Treatment is done twice or thrice weekly. Patient should wear protective eye goggles and apply sunscreens on untreated areas of face and neck. Side-effects might be redness, itching and peeling of skin, eye irritation, headache, phototoxic erythema etc.

It is one of the safest form of medical treatment for generalised and even unstable VITILIGO, and promises desired results. The cost of phototherapy in Bangalore varies depending on the condition you want to treat, but it is generally affordable.
