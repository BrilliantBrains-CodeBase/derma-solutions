## Frequently Asked Questions About Mole Removal

Get answers to common concerns about mole removal.

#### Is mole removal painful?

No, the procedure is done under local anesthesia, ensuring minimal to no discomfort.

#### Will mole removal leave scars?

Advanced techniques like RFN and CO2 laser minimize scarring, while surgical excision uses cosmetic techniques for the best results.

#### How long does the procedure take?

Most mole removal treatments are completed within 15–30 minutes.

#### Can all moles be removed?

Yes, but the method depends on the size, type, and location of the mole. A consultation will determine the best approach.

#### How soon will the treated area heal?

Healing typically occurs within 1–2 weeks for RFN or laser treatments, while surgical excisions may take slightly longer.

#### Can I resume normal activities after the treatment?

Yes, but it’s advisable to avoid direct sun exposure. Apply ointments prescribed by the doctor.

#### How does laser removal mole?

In the CO2 laser technique, the radiation’s heat works towards the mole removal, simultaneously sealing the skin to eliminate bleeding. As the surface has no scarring or wounds, this method is better for removing moles.

#### How does radio frequency ablation removes mole?

Ablation means the removal of something. Thus, radiofrequency ablation refers to the mole removal treatment using the heat of radio waves, having a frequency in the range of 350 to 500 kHz. Different forms of alternating current are used for this dermatological solution. It does not leave any wounds or scars behind and has faster healing.
