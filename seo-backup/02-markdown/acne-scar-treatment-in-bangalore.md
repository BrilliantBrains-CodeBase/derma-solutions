## What Causes Acne Scars?

Understand the root causes behind acne scars for better prevention and treatment.

### Causes of Acne

- **Severe Acne Breakouts :** Large, inflamed pimples or cysts can damage the skin’s structure.


- **Delayed or Improper Treatment :** Leaving acne untreated can lead to permanent scars.


- **Picking or Squeezing Acne :** Increases inflammation and damages the surrounding skin.


- **Genetics :** Some people are more prone to scarring due to their skin type.



### Different Types of Acne Scars

- **Rolling Scars :** Wavy, uneven scars that give the skin a rolling texture.


- **Boxcar Scars :** Broad, square-shaped scars with defined edges.


- **Ice Pick Scars :** Deep, narrow scars resembling small holes.


- **Hypertrophic Scars :** Raised scars caused by excess collagen production.

#### Laser Acne Scar Removal

Laser Scar Removal is one of the most popular treatments for acne scars. It uses high-energy laser beams to remove the outer layer of damaged skin and stimulate collagen production in the underlying layers, leading to smoother skin over time.

![Noun Facial Treatment 6939931 326f7d](https://dermasolutions.co.in/wp-content/uploads/2024/12/noun-facial-treatment-6939931-326F7D.svg)

#### Microneedling (MNRF)

This minimally invasive treatment involves creating tiny punctures in the skin with fine needles, encouraging the body’s natural healing process to boost collagen and elastin production. It is highly effective for improving the texture of acne-scarred skin.

![chemical peel icon](https://dermasolutions.co.in/wp-content/uploads/2024/12/noun-chemical-peel-7062960-326F7D.svg)

#### Chemical Peels

Chemical peels involve applying a solution to the skin that exfoliates the damaged layers and promotes new skin growth. This treatment is ideal for superficial acne scars and hyperpigmentation caused by acne.

![Botox icon](https://dermasolutions.co.in/wp-content/uploads/2024/12/noun-botox-7062974-326F7D.svg)

#### Derma Fillers

For depressed or indented scars, dermal fillers can be used to fill in the gaps, giving the skin a more even appearance. Results are usually immediate, although they may need to be repeated over time.

![Platelets](https://dermasolutions.co.in/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/noun-platelets-3627862-326F7D.svg)

#### PRP Therapy (Platelet-Rich Plasma)

PRP uses your body’s own platelets to accelerate the skin’s repair process. It can be combined with treatments like laser to boost results and promote faster healing.

## Frequently Asked Questions About Acne Scar Treatment

Get answers to your most common concerns.

#### How many sessions will I need?

The number of sessions depends on the type and severity of your scars. Most patients see noticeable improvement after 3-6 sessions, but our dermatologist will provide a customized treatment plan.

#### Is acne scar treatment painful?

Some procedures, like laser therapy may cause mild discomfort, but numbing creams are applied to minimize pain. Any post-treatment discomfort is usually manageable with over-the-counter pain relievers.

#### How soon will I see results?

Results typically begin to appear after 2–3 sessions and improve over time.

#### Can all acne scars be treated?

Yes, but the effectiveness varies depending on the type and severity of the scar.

#### Are the results permanent?

While treatments significantly reduce the appearance of scars, complete removal is not always possible, especially for deep scars. However, advanced treatments can greatly improve skin texture and tone, making scars less visible.

#### Are there any side affects?

Common side effects include redness, swelling, and mild discomfort, but these usually subside within a few days. Our dermatologists take all precautions to minimize any risks and will guide you through post-treatment care.
