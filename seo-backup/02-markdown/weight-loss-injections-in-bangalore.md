## How Do Weight Loss Injections Work?

**Science-Backed Injections That Help Regulate Appetite and Blood Sugar**

![Healthy Men](https://dermasolutions.co.in/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/ChatGPT-Image-Jun-6-2026-12_57_01-PM.png)

Weight loss injections work by mimicking a natural hormone in your body called GLP-1 (Glucagon-Like Peptide-1), which is responsible for regulating hunger and blood sugar.

When injected, this medication slows down your digestion, helps control blood sugar spikes, and sends signals to your brain that make you feel full faster and for longer.

Because of this, you naturally eat less without feeling starved or deprived. Over time, this reduced calorie intake leads to sustainable weight loss.

Many patients notice a significant drop in appetite within the first few weeks, followed by steady weight reduction over the next few months.

## FAQs About Weight Loss Treatment

**Get answers to your most common questions**

#### Are weight loss injections safe?

Yes, they are approved by global and Indian regulatory authorities and are prescribed by qualified doctors only after evaluating your medical history. When taken under medical supervision, they are considered safe for eligible individuals.

#### How much weight can I expect to lose?

Patients typically lose 5% to 15% of their body weight over a 3 to 6-month period, depending on consistency, lifestyle changes, and overall health status. Individual results may vary.

#### How long should I continue these injections?

Doctor usually recommend continuing for at least 3 to 6 months for noticeable results. Some may require longer treatment depending on weight loss goals and medical condition. Your doctor will decide the duration during your consultation.

#### Will I regain weight after stopping the injections?

Weight maintenance depends on your diet, activity level, and habits after stopping the injection. That’s why we offer holistic care—including lifestyle guidance and diet plans—to ensure long-term success.

#### Do I need to follow a diet while taking the injection?

Yes. While the injection helps suppress appetite, combining it with a healthy diet and active lifestyle improves results significantly. Our experts will guide you with a personalized food and fitness plan.
