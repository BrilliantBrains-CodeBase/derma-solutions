## What Causes Hair Loss?

Explore the common triggers behind hair thinning and hair fall.

Hair loss can be caused by various factors, ranging from genetics to lifestyle choices.

Identifying the root cause is the first step in creating an effective treatment plan.

**Common Causes of Hair Loss:**

#### Androgenetic Alopecia (Pattern Baldness)

Hereditary hair loss in men and women.

![Hair Loss](https://dermasolutions.co.in/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/noun-hair-loss-6405656-326F7D.svg)

#### Telogen Effluvium

Temporary shedding due to stress, illness, or hormonal changes.

![Alopecia](https://dermasolutions.co.in/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/noun-alopecia-5607239-326F7D.svg)

#### Alopecia Areata

Autoimmune condition causing patchy hair loss.

#### Nutritional Deficiencies

Lack of essential nutrients like iron, zinc, or biotin.

![Dandruff](https://dermasolutions.co.in/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/noun-dandruff-7382480-326F7D.svg)

#### Scalp Infections and Conditions

Dandruff, psoriasis, or fungal infections affecting hair growth.

![Lifestyle icon](https://dermasolutions.co.in/wp-content/uploads/2024/12/noun-lifestyle-7322545-326F7D.svg)

#### Lifestyle Factors

Stress, pollution, and overuse of styling products.
