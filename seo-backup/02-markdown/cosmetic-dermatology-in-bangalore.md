# Cosmetic Dermatology in Bangalore

Cosmetic Dermatology is the most sought after branch of medical care as it focuses on beauty and appearance. Today’s generation are quite conscious of their natural beauty as the first impression is created by good looks and appearance.

Hence in order to improve or enhance the texture of the skin, reach out to the best cosmetologist who provides the best solution for a great look.

### The skin gets damaged due to many factors which an individual faces as follows:

- Injury marks
- Burns
- Sunlight
- Pollution
- Weight loss and gain (Causes stretch marks)
- Use of skin care products that are not prescribed by the doctors.

Derma solutions is one of the leading dermatology clinics in Bangalore providing services for cosmetic treatments, skin and hair problems. There are specified procedures and treatments for all types of skin to enhance the appearance. The therapies that are included in cosmetic dermatology are Botox and Dysport injections that is used to reduce wrinkles and facial lines, chemical peels to improve the appearance of the skin, Laser hair removal, laser therapy and microneedling etc. These treatments help solve any kind of skin related issues and helps one retain back your confidence.

Cosmetic Dermatology is also helpful for reduction of **acne scars**, stretch marks, age spots and lines and wrinkles. The natural looking, healthy looking youthful skin can be maintained by using appropriate medical skin care products prescribed by qualified Cosmetologist.

Cosmetologists are trained to perform cosmetic treatments and surgeries and Derma Solutions is one of the best Clinics for cosmetic treatments in Bangalore.
