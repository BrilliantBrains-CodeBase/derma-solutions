## What to Expect During Your Hair Transplant Procedure

A step-by-step guide to your NFT hair transplant journey.

### Step 1: Consultation and Assessment

Scalp analysis and discussion of goals with our hair transplant specialists.

![Tailored Treatment icon](https://dermasolutions.co.in/wp-content/uploads/2024/12/noun-treatment-7409904-326F7D.svg)

### Step 2: Planning and Preparation

Custom design of the hairline and donor area evaluation.

![Hair Transplant](https://dermasolutions.co.in/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/noun-hair-transplant-6440976-326F7D.svg)

### Step 3: NFT Extraction

Individual follicular units are extracted with precision to ensure survival. The hair follicular units are extracted one by one from the donor area by using the most advanced semi-automated instruments with a diameter of 0.7mm to 0.8mm.

![Skin Care](https://dermasolutions.co.in/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/noun-skin-care-6759110-326F7D.svg)

### Step 4: Preparation of Scalp

The scalp is first prepared first by infusing GFT (Growth Factor Therapy) before implanting the follicles. The growth factor present in the semipermeable membrane will act as a booster to the transplanted hair follicles.

The extracted hair follicle are segregated and preserved in a liquid solution under a specific temperature which enhances the growth after the implantation.

![Noun Hair Transplant 7103592 326f7d](https://dermasolutions.co.in/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/noun-hair-transplant-7103592-326F7D.svg)

### Step 5: Graft Implantation

The hair follicle are implanted directly using NFT implanter by the hair transplant surgeon. This implanter will enable the surgeon to have complete control on the angle, depth and direction of the follicle to gain complete natural results with maximum density.

Hair follicles are carefully implanted to create a natural hairline and density.

![Comprehensive care icon](https://dermasolutions.co.in/wp-content/uploads/2024/12/noun-care-7368620-326F7D.svg)

### Step 6: Post-Treatment Care

Detailed aftercare instructions for optimal recovery and results.
