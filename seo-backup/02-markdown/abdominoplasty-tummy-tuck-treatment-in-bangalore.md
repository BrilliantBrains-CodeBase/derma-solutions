## Frequently Asked Questions About Tummy Tuck Surgery

Get all your questions answered before your procedure.

#### Is tummy tuck surgery painful?

The procedure is performed under anesthesia, so you won’t feel pain during surgery. Post-operative pain is manageable with medications.

#### How long does recovery take?

Most patients recover within 4–6 weeks, but it’s important to follow post-operative care instructions.

#### Will the results be permanent?

Yes, with a stable weight and healthy lifestyle, the results of abdominoplasty are long-lasting.

#### Are there visible scars after the procedure?

Scars are carefully placed in inconspicuous areas and fade over time with proper care.
