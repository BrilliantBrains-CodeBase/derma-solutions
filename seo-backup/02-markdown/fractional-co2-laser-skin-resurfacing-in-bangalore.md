## How Does the Fractional CO2 Laser Work?

Stimulating collagen production for smoother, firmer skin.

The Fractional CO2 Laser works by delivering precise laser beams into the skin’s deeper layers, creating thousands of microscopic wounds. These micro-injuries trigger the body's natural healing process, stimulating collagen and elastin production, leading to tighter, younger-looking skin.

This process removes damaged skin layers and stimulates new cell growth, replacing old skin with healthier, firmer, and brighter skin.
