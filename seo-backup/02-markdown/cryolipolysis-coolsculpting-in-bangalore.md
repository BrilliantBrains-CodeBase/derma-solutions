## How Does Cryolipolysis / Cool Sculpting Work?

The science behind freezing away stubborn fat.

Cryolipolysis uses controlled cooling technology to target and freeze fat cells beneath the skin. These fat cells crystallize and are naturally eliminated by the body over the following weeks, leaving the surrounding tissues unaffected.

The result is a slimmer, more contoured appearance without the need for invasive surgery.
