## What is Gynecomastia?

A common condition causing enlargement of male breast tissue.

Gynecomastia is a condition characterized by an enlargement of male breast tissue due to hormonal imbalances, genetics, or certain medications.

Gynecomastia surgery, also known as male breast reduction, is a proven surgical solution to address this issue and restore a flat, masculine chest contour.

## Frequently Asked Questions About Gynecomastia Surgery

Answers to your most common queries about male breast reduction.

#### Is gynecomastia surgery painful?

The procedure is performed under anesthesia, so there is no pain during surgery. Post-operative discomfort is mild and manageable with medication.

#### How long does recovery take?

Most patients recover within 1–2 weeks, with full results visible in 3–6 months.

#### Will there be visible scars?

Small, inconspicuous scars fade over time with proper care.

#### Can gynecomastia return after surgery?

The results are permanent, but maintaining a healthy lifestyle is crucial to prevent recurrence.

#### Is gynecomastia surgery safe?

Yes, it is a safe and effective procedure when performed by experienced surgeons.
