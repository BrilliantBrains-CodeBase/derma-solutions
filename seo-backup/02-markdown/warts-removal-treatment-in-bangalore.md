## What Causes Warts?

Understanding the root cause of warts helps in effective treatment and prevention

### What Causes Warts?

Warts are caused by certain strains of the human papillomavirus (HPV), which infects the top layer of skin.

The virus enters through small cuts or abrasions, leading to abnormal growth of skin cells.

They spread via contact with infected source and also sexually sometimes in genital area. Warts are very recurrent in nature.

Warts are more common in individuals with weakened immune systems or those frequently exposed to moist environments.

### Type of Warts

- **Common Warts :** Appear on hands and fingers; rough and dome-shaped.


- **Plantar Warts :** Found on the soles of the feet; often painful due to pressure.


- **Flat Warts :** Smaller, smoother warts that commonly appear on the face, arms, or legs.

- **Filiform Warts :** Long, thread-like warts usually found on the face


- **Genital Warts :** Appear in the genital area and require specialized treatment.
