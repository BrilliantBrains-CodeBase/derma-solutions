## How Does Growth Factor Concentrate Treatment Work?

The science behind natural healing and rejuvenation.

GFC Treatment involves isolating growth factors from your own blood through a special centrifugation process. These growth factors are then injected into targeted areas of the scalp or skin, where they stimulate cell repair, boost collagen production, and improve blood flow.

![Gfc Hair Treatment Injection](https://dermasolutions.co.in/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/GFC-Hair-Treatment-injection.jpg)

A small amount of blood is drawn from your arm.

### Step 2: Centrifugation

The blood is processed to extract highly concentrated growth factors.

## GFC vs. PRP: What’s the Difference?

**An advanced alternative to PRP therapy**

While both GFC and PRP (Platelet-Rich Plasma) treatments use growth factors from your blood, GFC offers enhanced results due to higher concentration and purity.

GFC eliminates unnecessary cells, focusing solely on active growth factors for superior outcomes in hair and skin rejuvenation.

**Key Differences:**

• Concentration: GFC contains a higher concentration of growth factors than PRP.

• Effectiveness: Delivers faster and more noticeable results.

• Comfort: GFC involves fewer injections and less discomfort.
