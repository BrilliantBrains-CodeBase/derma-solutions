## Frequently Asked Questions

**Get answers to your most common concerns.**

### Are the results of glutathione IV drip permanent?

No, the results are not permanent. With maintenance sessions and a healthy lifestyle, you can sustain the glow for longer, but stopping treatment may gradually reduce the effects.

### Can anyone take glutathione IV injections?

Not everyone is an ideal candidate. A doctor’s consultation is necessary, especially if you have medical conditions, are pregnant, or are on medication.

### Is glutathione a fairness treatment?

No, glutathione is not a “fairness treatment.” It works by improving skin health, reducing pigmentation, and enhancing natural radiance — not by changing your natural skin tone.

### How long do glutathione IV results last?

Results can last for a few months depending on your skin, lifestyle, and maintenance sessions. Regular follow-ups help maintain consistent results.

### Can glutathione IV therapy help with detox?

Yes, glutathione plays a key role in detoxification by helping the liver remove toxins and reducing oxidative stress in the body.

### Is glutathione IV drip suitable for men?

Absolutely. Glutathione therapy is suitable for both men and women looking to improve skin clarity, reduce pigmentation, and boost overall wellness.

### Can I combine glutathione IV with other skin treatments?

Yes, it can be combined with treatments like facials, peels, or laser procedures for enhanced results, depending on your doctor’s recommendation.
