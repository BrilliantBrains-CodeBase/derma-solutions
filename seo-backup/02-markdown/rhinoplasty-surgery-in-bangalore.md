## Frequently Asked Questions About Rhinoplasty Surgery

Answers to your most common queries about nose reshaping.

### Is rhinoplasty surgery painful?

The procedure is performed under anesthesia, so there is no pain during surgery. Mild discomfort during recovery can be managed with medication.

#### How long does recovery take?

Most patients recover within 2–3 weeks, with swelling and bruising subsiding over time.

#### Will there be visible scars?

In closed rhinoplasty, there are no visible scars. Open rhinoplasty may leave a small scar that fades over time.

#### Can rhinoplasty improve breathing?

Yes, functional rhinoplasty can correct structural issues like a deviated septum to improve nasal airflow.

#### Are the results permanent?

Yes, the results of rhinoplasty are permanent, but it’s essential to follow post-surgery care instructions.
