# Inch Reduction Treatment In Bangalore

Inch reduction is a process of weight loss whereby the weight reduction is more focused on losing weight or inches from a specific part of the body where he/she has accumulated fat. Every individual wish to maintain his/her body shape in a good condition.

This treatment is non-surgical.

There is no side effects and no heavy exercise that is required to be done.

The excess fat from the specific area is treated with the use of high-end technology, whereby the fat is targeted and expelled.

The techniques used for inch reduction is “Ultrasonic Lipolysis” and “Radio frequency Skin Tightening”.

### Ultrasonic Lipolysis

It is a non-surgical treatment to get rid of fat. Ultrasound waves are used to expel the excess accumulated fat.

Then a transducer is placed on the affected area where it emits energy.

These fat cells are metabolised by the body and are drained out, that helps in weight loss.

### Radio Frequency Skin Tightening

This technique induces tissues remodelling and production of new collagen and elastin.

This process is an alternative to facelift and other cosmetic surgeries.

Radio Frequency is used for heating and reducing the fat. In the current period, most radiofrequency devices are to noninvasively manage and treat skin tightening as well as reduce wrinkles and fat reduction.
