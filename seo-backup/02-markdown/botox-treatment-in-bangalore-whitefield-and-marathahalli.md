## Frequently Asked Questions About Botox Treatment

Get all your questions answered before your session.

#### How long does Botox last?

Botox results typically last 3–6 months, depending on individual factors and the treatment area.

#### Is Botox painful?

The procedure involves minimal discomfort, often compared to a small pinprick.

#### How soon will I see results?

Initial results are visible within 3–5 days, with full effects appearing after two weeks.

#### Can Botox prevent wrinkles?

Yes, Botox relaxes facial muscles, preventing the formation of new wrinkles over time.

#### Is there any downtime after Botox?

No, Botox has no downtime, and you can resume normal activities immediately.

#### Are there any side affects?

Botox is a safe and FDA-approved procedure, but it may have mild, temporary side effects such as redness, swelling, or bruising at the injection site, as well as occasional headaches or slight discomfort. Rarely, temporary eyelid drooping or flu-like symptoms may occur. These effects typically resolve within a few hours to days. Proper aftercare, such as avoiding rubbing the treated area and following your dermatologist's instructions, can help minimize these risks.
