## Frequently Asked Questions About Dermatology Care

Your skin and hair deserve the best care.

#### What conditions do dermatologists treat?

Dermatologists treat a wide range of skin, hair, and nail conditions including acne, eczema, psoriasis, rosacea, hair loss, pigmentation disorders, and skin infections. They also provide cosmetic services like anti-aging treatments, skin rejuvenation, and hair restoration.

#### When should I see a dermatologist?

If you’re experiencing persistent skin issues like acne, unusual rashes, severe hair loss, or changes in your skin that don’t resolve with over-the-counter treatments, it’s time to consult a dermatologist for a professional evaluation.

#### What should I expect during my first dermatologist visit?

During your first visit, the dermatologist will review your medical history, discuss your skin concerns, and perform a skin examination. They may recommend treatments or further tests to diagnose your condition and create a personalized care plan.

#### Are cosmetic treatment safe?

Yes, when performed by a qualified dermatologist, cosmetic treatments such as Botox, dermal fillers, and laser therapy are safe and effective. Dr Sandeep Mahapatra, our dermatologists prioritizes patient safety and ensure all procedures are done following the highest medical standards.

#### Do dermatologists treat hair and scalp problems?

Yes, dermatologists are trained to diagnose and treat a variety of hair and scalp conditions including dandruff, hair thinning, alopecia, and other scalp disorders.

#### What is the cost of a consultation with a dermatologist?

Consultation fees vary depending on the clinic and the services required. It’s best to contact us directly for detailed pricing information and any package options available.

#### How often should I visit a dermatologist?

It’s recommended to visit a dermatologist at least once a year for a routine skin check. However, if you have specific skin concerns or are undergoing treatment, more frequent visits may be necessary.

#### Can I get same-day appointments for urgent skin conditions?

We strive to accommodate urgent cases as quickly as possible. Contact our clinic for availability and we’ll do our best to schedule a same-day appointment for pressing skin concerns.
