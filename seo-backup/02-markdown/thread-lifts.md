## How Does Thread Lift Treatment Work?

The science behind this non-surgical skin-lifting procedure.

Thread lift treatment involves inserting dissolvable threads into the skin using fine needles. These threads are strategically placed to lift sagging skin and create a natural, youthful contour. Over time, the threads dissolve, leaving behind a scaffold of collagen that continues to support and firm the skin.

## Frequently Asked Questions About Thread Lift Treatment

Get answers to your most common questions.

#### Is thread lift treatment painful?

Most patients experience minimal discomfort during the procedure. Local anesthesia is used to ensure a pain-free experience.

#### How long does the procedure take?

A typical session lasts 30–60 minutes, depending on the area treated.

#### How long do the results last?

Thread lift results vary based on the type of threads used and individual skin factors. On average, results can last from 12 to 24 months.

#### Are thread lifts suitable for all skin types?

Yes, thread lifts are suitable for all skin types. Our practitioners assess your skin condition and tailor the procedure accordingly.

#### How to Prepare for thread lift treatment?

Avoid alcohol and blood-thinning medications for 48 hours before the procedure. Discuss your medical history and skincare routine with your dermatologist.

#### How to Prepare for thread lift after treatment?

Avoid strenuous activities, facial massages, or excessive facial movements for 1–2 weeks. Apply cold compresses to reduce swelling if needed. Follow the skincare routine recommended by our dermatologist.

#### Are there any side effects?

Mild swelling, bruising, or redness may occur but typically resolves within a few days.

#### Can thread lift be combined with other treatments?

Yes, thread lift can be combined with fillers, Botox, or skin tightening for enhanced results.
