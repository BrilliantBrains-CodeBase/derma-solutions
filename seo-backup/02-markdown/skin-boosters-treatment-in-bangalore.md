## What Are Skin Boosters?

What Are Skin Boosters & How Do They Work?

![Derma SKin Booster Injection](https://dermasolutions.co.in/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/Derma-SKin-Booster-Injection.png)

Skin boosters are micro-injections of hyaluronic acid, vitamins, peptides, and antioxidants directly into the skin.

Unlike dermal fillers which add volume, skin boosters hydrate and improve skin texture from the inside out. They work by deeply moisturizing the skin, stimulating collagen production, and restoring elasticity.

Think of them as long-lasting internal moisturizers — perfect for dry, aging, sun-damaged, or fatigued skin.

## Skin Booster Treatment Process

What to Expect During the Skin Booster Session

Our dermatologist evaluates your skin condition and recommends the right type of booster.

![mole on Skin icon](https://dermasolutions.co.in/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/noun-skin-6071866-326F7D.svg)

### Step 2: Cleansing & Numbing

Area is cleansed and a topical numbing cream is applied to reduce discomfort.

![Laser icon](https://dermasolutions.co.in/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/noun-laser-6939949-326F7D.svg)

### Step 3: Micro-Injections

Tiny quantities of boosters are injected into the mid-dermis using fine needles.

![Skin Care](https://dermasolutions.co.in/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/noun-skin-care-6759110-326F7D.svg)

### Step 4: Post Care

A soothing cream is applied. You may have slight redness or swelling that resolves in a few hours.

![Confidence](https://dermasolutions.co.in/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/noun-confidence-6602266-326F7D.svg)

### Step 5: Follow-Up

Sessions are spaced 3–4 weeks apart; 3 sessions are typically recommended for best results.

#### Pre - Treatment Guidelines

- Avoid alcohol and blood thinners for 24–48 hours

- No active breakouts or infections in the area

- Do not apply retinol 2–3 days before treatment


#### Post - Treatment Guidelines

- Avoid touching, rubbing, or applying makeup for 12 hours

- Avoid sun exposure and heavy exercise for 24–48 hours

- Apply cold compress if minor swelling appears
