## How Does the Hollywood Facial Work?

Step-by-step guide to glowing, rejuvenated skin.

![Laser machines at Derma Solutions](https://dermasolutions.co.in/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/Machine.jpg)

![Carbon Peel](https://dermasolutions.co.in/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/noun-carbon-peel-7062946-326F7D.svg)

### Step 1: Application of Carbon Solution

A thin layer of carbon liquid is applied to your skin, which absorbs oil, dirt, and impurities from deep within the pores.

![Laser icon](https://dermasolutions.co.in/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/noun-laser-7013279-326F7D.svg)

### Step 2: Laser Activation

A specialized laser is passed over the skin, targeting the carbon particles. The laser vaporizes the carbon, removing impurities, dead skin, and excess oil.

![Skin Care](https://dermasolutions.co.in/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/noun-skin-care-6759110-326F7D.svg)

### Step 3: Skin Rejuvenation

The heat generated during the process stimulates collagen production, tightening the skin and reducing fine lines and wrinkles.

![](https://dermasolutions.co.in/wp-content/uploads/2024/12/noun-facial-treatment-6601445-326F7D.svg)

### Step 4: Instant Glow

The treatment ends with your skin feeling smoother, brighter, and rejuvenated.
