## **Got questions? We've got answers\!**

### **1\. Is glutathione IV drip treatment safe?**

When administered under appropriate medical supervision, IV therapy is monitored throughout the session. Mild nausea, headache or discomfort at the injection site may occur in some individuals.

### **2\. Who should avoid glutathione IV drips?**

Your doctor will review your medical history, allergies, existing health conditions and suitability before recommending an IV drip. A skin test or other basic checks may be advised where appropriate.

### **3\. How long do the results last?**

Results vary from person to person and depend on individual skin concerns, lifestyle and the treatment protocol. Maintenance sessions may be recommended depending on your response and goals.

### **4\. Is glutathione IV drip suitable for men?**

Yes. **Glutathione IV drip treatment** may be considered for men as well as women, depending on their individual skin and wellness goals and medical suitability.
