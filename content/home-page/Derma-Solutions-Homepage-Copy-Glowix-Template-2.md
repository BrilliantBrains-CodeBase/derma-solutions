## **COMPLIANCE NOTES BEFORE THIS GOES LIVE**

1\. Numeric claims (20+ / 50,000+ / 10,000+ / 4 specialists). Under ASCI substantiation rules these must be backed by clinic records if challenged. Confirm the figures with Dr Sandeep before publishing. The 40,000+ chemical peels and 60,000+ PRP figures on the current site should get the same check if you use them anywhere else.

2\. Before & After images. Use only the clinic's own images with signed patient consent on file. Do not use stock or theme demo images as patient results. Keep the "Results vary from person to person" line visible next to the images.

3\. "Botox" in the Services card. It's a prescription drug brand name. Naming it on a clinic site is common practice but sits close to the Drugs and Magic Remedies Act line. Safer alternative if you want zero exposure: "Anti-wrinkle injections, dermal fillers, HIFU and thread lifts."

4\. "Get a Free Assessment." Only run this if the consultation genuinely is free. If it's a paid consultation, change to "Book a Consultation."

5\. Google rating in the hero. Do not hardcode a number that isn't current. Pull the live rating or use a static "Rated highly by patients across Bangalore."

6\. E-E-A-T gap worth flagging to the client. The template has no doctor-profile section, so MD credentials, the Gold Medalist qualification, DHI certification and the Allergan certification now appear nowhere on the homepage. For a medical site that's a real trust and search-visibility loss. Recommend adding a compact 4-doctor strip between "Why Choose Us" and "How It Works" \- the theme's team block or a simple custom row would do it.
